WikiFlow — Wikipedia Clickstream BDA
Analysis Notes

Dataset
- Analysis month: September 2026 (2026-09).
- Source: cleaned Wikipedia clickstream data.
- Main cleaned dataset: clean/edges.
- Additional datasets: clean/main_page and clean/sources.
- Internal article transitions are identified using type = link.

A1 — Article and traffic-source analysis
- Generated the top 20 destination articles by aggregated incoming click counts.
- Generated total click counts by source label.
- Output files: a1_top_articles.csv, traffic_sources.csv.
- Main_Page has the highest aggregated incoming click count in the current results.

A2 — Transition analysis
- Generated the top 20 transitions by click count.
- Generated the top 20 transitions by probability, with n >= 1000.
- Output files: a2_top_by_count.csv, a2_top_by_prob.csv.

### A3 — Two-way article pairs and graph communities

- Generated `a3_two_way.csv` with 2,000 two-way article pairs.
- Generated `a3_clusters.csv` using Louvain community detection with NetworkX.
- Clustering input: the graph constructed from the 2,000 selected two-way pairs, with `total_clicks` used as edge weight.
- Output: 2,538 unique article assignments across 651 communities.
- Community sizes range from 2 to 64 articles.
- Limitation: these are communities in the selected subgraph, not clusters derived from the complete Wikipedia clickstream graph. Many communities are small, so topic coherence is not guaranteed.
A4 — Estimated two-step paths
- Generated the top 50 estimated paths using aggregated transition counts.
- Output file: a4_paths.csv.
- These values are estimates derived from aggregate counts. They do not represent reconstructed individual user sessions.

A5 — Article subgraphs
- Extracted incoming and outgoing internal links around four selected starting articles.
- Output files:
  - a5_subgraph_asian_games.csv
  - a5_subgraph_artificial_intelligence.csv
  - a5_subgraph_india.csv
  - a5_subgraph_python.csv

Data-quality checks
- Cleaned data was inspected for nulls and invalid click counts.
- Duplicate transition keys and probability normalization were checked.
- See reports/data_quality.md and reports/graph_summary.md for the existing validation reports.

Limitations
- Clickstream counts represent aggregated transitions, not complete browsing sessions.
- Two-step path estimates are approximate.
- Article grouping by initial character should not be interpreted as semantic topic discovery.
- Results should be interpreted within the scope of the supplied September 2026 dataset.
